# Product Research — Outdoor Adventure Superstore

Metrics used: category market-size & growth data, cross-platform best-seller overlap (Amazon/AliExpress/Walmart/CJdropshipping trend lists), and Google Trends seasonal search-volume signals.

**Brand frame:** Basecamp & Backwoods = gear for trail, water, range day, and weekend survival — not a single-sport specialty shop.

## Market signals

- **Camping / outdoor equipment:** large, durable demand; year-round + spring–summer peak.
- **Water sports accessories:** strong seasonal (Apr–Sep) with high impulse AOV (dry bags, PFDs-adjacent accessories, paddles, float kits).
- **Survival / preparedness:** steady search interest; gifting + storm season spikes; pairs with camping.
- **Shooting-sports *accessories* (not firearms/ammo):** range bags, hearing protection, targets, bipods, cleaning kits — large US market when kept accessory-only for shipping/compliance.
- **Outdoor apparel & tools:** layering, gloves, belts, multitools — high attach-rate upsells.
- **Cross-platform trend overlap** remains the strongest durable-demand signal.

## Compliance / do-not-list (hard)

- Firearms, ammunition, high-capacity magazines, illegal weapons, explosives.
- Obvious weapons marketed for violence; age-restricted where store policy requires.
- IP/brand knockoffs (logos, licensed characters).
- Heavy hazmat / lithium edge cases that break CJ shipping rules without review.

## Decision process (product picks)

1. **Niche map** — product must fit a collection above.  
2. **Ship / compliance** — drop if restricted or high-return electronics without clear fit.  
3. **Hero economics** — light/small preferred; target retail at **2.5–3.5×** CJ cost.  
4. **CJ match** — tracked inventory required; Must/Prefer/Reject name score ≥ 10.  
5. **Merchandising** — Best Sellers = curated cross-niche mix, not raw import order.

## Sourcing notes (CJdropshipping)

- Prefer US/EU warehouse / CJ Packet when available.  
- Rating ≥4.5 and visible order volume when possible.  
- Sample top survival, ear-pro, and water SKUs before heavy ads (human purchase).  
- For shooting accessories: double-check platform + carrier rules per destination.
