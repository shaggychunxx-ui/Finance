## 1. What these standards are

Meta’s **Advertising Standards** define:

- What **ad content** is allowed vs prohibited  
- **Advertiser behavior** that can restrict a Business Account or its assets (**ad account, Page, user account**)  

Every ad order is reviewed against these policies. Ads must also follow **Community Standards**.

**Principles (Meta’s framing):**

1. Protect people from **unsafe and discriminatory** practices  
2. Protect people from **fraud / scams**  
3. Promote **positive user experiences** (no shocking/sensational/excessively violent content; limits on adult content/profanity; no negative self-perception hooks for diet/weight-loss style ads)  
4. Promote **transparency** (Ad Library; Page “Info and Ads”; SIEP ads extra disclosure)

**Enforcement:** automated + sometimes manual review. Ads can be **re-reviewed after live** and rejected later. Lower-quality ads (even if not banned) can get **worse delivery**. Violations can restrict **Business / ad account / Page / user** advertising rights.

## 2. Ad review process (agent expectations)

| Step | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| Start | Review usually starts **before** ads run; status **“In review”** |
| Timing | Often **within 24 hours**, sometimes longer |
| Scope | Images, video, text, **targeting**, **landing page / destinations**, other signals |
| Asset review | Business Account and assets can be assessed separately |
| Outcome | Ad **rejected** and/or assets **restricted** from advertising |
| After live | **Re-review anytime** — compliance is ongoing |

**If rejected / restricted:**

1. **Edit** the ad to comply (or create a new compliant ad) → goes through review again  
2. Or **request another review** in [Account Quality](https://www.facebook.com/accountquality) if you believe it was wrong  

**Agent rule:** Do not spam-resubmit the same non-compliant creative. Fix claims, landing page match, or restricted product issues first. Document rejection reason in STATUS / task Result.

## 3. High-risk categories for Basecamp (outdoor dropship)

### Must never promote in ads (especially relevant)

| Category | Policy gist | Basecamp note |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Weapons, ammunition, explosives** | No sale/use of weapons, ammo, explosives, **weapon modification accessories**; includes many self-defense weapons (e.g. pepper spray, non-culinary knives/blades as weapons, tasers, etc.) | Catalog may have **utility knives** — **do not ad** weaponized framing; prefer zero knife/weapon ads until human clears policy fit |
| **Tobacco / nicotine / vapes** | No sale/use of tobacco, nicotine, paraphernalia, e-cigs/vapes | Not in Week 1 plan — stay away |
| **Illicit / recreational / unsafe drugs** | No promoting sale/use; Rx/OTC/cannabis-derived have special rules | No supplement/“miracle cure” ads |
| **Counterfeit / IP infringement** | No ads that violate copyright/trademark; **no counterfeit goods** | Supplier images/titles must not fake brands |
| **Discriminatory practices** | No discriminatory content or wrongful target/exclude by protected attributes; housing/employment/credit → Special Ad Category rules | US outdoor ecom: use broad interest targeting per plan; don’t exclude protected classes to “improve ROAS” |
| **Misinformation** | No content debunked by fact-checkers; repeated falsehoods → ad restrictions | Claims on filter/water must be **honest** and match PDP |
| **Fraud / unacceptable business practices** | No deceptive schemes; suspicious categories may need verification | No fake freebies, fake urgency, bait-and-switch |
| **Personal attributes in ad text** | Ads must not assert/imply user’s race, health, finances, name, etc. | Avoid “Are you tired of being broke/overweight?” style copy |
| **Profanity / graphic violence / adult content** | Prohibited or heavily restricted | Keep creatives trail/outdoor clean |
| **Weapons-adjacent “tactical” hype** | Can trip restricted goods or shocking content | Frame **camping / hiking / outdoor** not combat |

### Restricted (extra rules; often age-gate or permission)

Examples: alcohol, health/weight-loss claims, dating (permission), gambling (permission), crypto trading (permission), financial products (18+, no PII capture in ad), etc. **Week 1 hero is not these categories** — don’t expand into them without human + policy check.

### Health & wellness (watch for supplements / body image)

Weight-loss/gain, cosmetic procedures, dietary/health supplements have targeting and claim limits; negative body/inferiority language is banned. **Don’t** market outdoor gear with body-shaming hooks.

## 4. Privacy, personal attributes, lead ads

**Ad copy:** Must not share or ask for private user info; must not assert/imply personal attributes (race, religion, age, orientation, disability, health, vulnerable finances, voting status, etc.).

**Lead ads:** Without prior written permission, do **not** request sensitive fields (account numbers, criminal history, financial details, gov IDs, health, insurance, political affiliation, race/ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, usernames/passwords, trade union membership, etc.). Prefer website conversion / clicks to store for Basecamp.

**Lead Ad Terms (separate legal doc):** https://www.facebook.com/legal/leadgen/tos — team summary **`notes/meta-lead-ad-terms.md`**. If Lead Generation Features are used: no minors, required disclosures, purpose-limited use, **never sell** leads, security + Platform Terms for APIs. Week 1 default remains **Shopify PDP**, not lead forms.

## 5. Targeting, relevance, landing page

1. **No predatory / discriminatory targeting** — don’t use targeting to harass, disparage, or wrongfully exclude protected groups.  
2. **Custom audiences** → comply with Custom Audience terms.  
3. **Relevance:** Ad must clearly represent the brand/product; all components relevant to the offer.  
4. **Landing page match:** Products/services in the ad must match the landing page.  
   - Week 1: only **Zendrop** water filter straw PDP — not CJ twin, not homepage bait.

## 6. Data use restrictions (critical for agents)

From Meta Advertising Standards (data use):

1. Share Meta advertising data only with people acting on your behalf (service providers); keep it confidential/secure.  
2. Use Meta ad data only to assess campaign performance/effectiveness, generally **aggregate and anonymous** (unless Meta authorizes otherwise). **No** retargeting/commingling/piggybacking tags beyond allowed use.  
3. **Do not** use Meta ad data (including targeting criteria) to build/append/edit **user profiles** tied to device IDs or unique identifiers.  
4. **Do not** transfer Meta advertising data to ad networks, exchanges, data brokers, or other monetization services.

**Agent rule:** Pixel + API insights stay in Meta/Shopify approved tools. No exporting audiences to random third parties or building off-platform shadow profiles from Meta data.

## 7. Business assets & integrity

Restrictions can hit **Business Account, ad account, Page, user** for:

- Account integrity / inauthentic behavior  
- Cybersecurity (no phishing, malware links)  
- Spam  
- Misrepresentation / suspicious activity → may require **verification**  

**Agent rules:**

- One legitimate business identity (Basecamp and Backwoods)  
- Do not buy/sell admin access or help evade enforcement  
- Do not create sock-puppet ad accounts to dodge bans  
- If restricted → human + Account Quality; do not “work around”

## 9. Things Meta wants advertisers to know (summary)

- Policies apply to ads on/off Meta via their tools, apps, Instagram, etc.  
- Use of ads is under **Terms of Service** + these standards.  
- Advertiser is responsible for **local law** compliance.  
- Ads become **public**; may be reshared; political ads go to Ad Library for years.  
- Meta may reject/remove ads for any reason in their discretion (including competitive/position reasons).  
- Policies **change without notice** — re-check Transparency Center before major launches.

## 10. ShopifyDS agent checklist (before any ad create/launch)

1. Read this note + official https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/  
2. Creative + copy: truthful, no profanity, no personal-attribute callouts, no fake urgency/scams  
3. Product: **not** weapons/ammo, tobacco, illicit drugs, counterfeits  
4. Landing page = exact promoted SKU (Week 1 Zendrop straw PDP)  
5. Targeting: plan audiences only; no discriminatory exclusion hacks  
6. Budget: ≤ $25/wk / ~$3.50/day; see budget note  
7. Page + durable token + creatives still required before launch (`STATUS.md`)  
8. On reject: fix root cause → resubmit or Account Quality; log result  
9. Never transfer Meta ad data to brokers/networks  
10. Human for: special ad categories, restricted goods permission, identity verification, legal disputes
