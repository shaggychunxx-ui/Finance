# Shopify Store Setup Plan — "Outdoors & Overland Co." (working name)

A step-by-step build checklist so the human (or a future agent given real Shopify/CJ credentials) can execute this quickly and professionally. Replace bracketed placeholders with your real brand name/domain.

## 1. Brand basics
- **Store name:** e.g. "Basecamp & Backroads", "Trailhead Supply Co.", "Overland & Ember" (pick one; check domain availability)
- **Tagline:** "Gear for the trail and the tailgate."
- **Color palette:** forest green / charcoal / burnt-orange accent (rugged, outdoor-professional look)
- **Logo:** use Shopify's free Hatchful logo maker or Canva; keep it simple (icon + wordmark)
- **Domain:** buy via Shopify domains or connect an existing one

## 2. Shopify theme
- Use a **free Shopify theme** to start (no unnecessary spend): **Dawn**, **Craft**, or **Sense** — all support large hero images, collection grids, and are mobile-optimized.
- Upgrade later to a paid theme (e.g., "Impulse" or "Prestige") once sales validate the niche.

## 3. Store structure (pages/navigation)

**Main nav:** Home · Shop All · Camping & Outdoor · Grilling & Cooking · Overland & 4x4 · About · FAQ/Shipping · Contact

- **Home** — hero banner (lifestyle photo: campsite/grill/off-road), 3 featured collections, best-sellers grid, trust badges (secure checkout, US-based support, 30-day returns), email signup for 10% off first order.
- **Collections:** Camping & Outdoor Gear · Grilling & Outdoor Cooking · Overland & Off-Road Accessories · Bundles & Kits · Best Sellers · New Arrivals
- **About** — short brand story: founded by outdoor enthusiasts, mission = quality gear for weekend adventures, without the premium markup.
- **FAQ / Shipping & Returns** — set honest expectations for dropshipping lead times (typically 7–15 business days from CJ warehouses; note faster options for CJ Packet/US-warehouse items).
- **Contact** — contact form + support email.
- **Legal pages** (Shopify auto-generates drafts): Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Refund Policy, Shipping Policy — review/edit before publishing.

## 4. Homepage copy (ready to paste)

**Hero headline:** "Gear Built for the Trail, the Tailgate, and Everywhere In Between"
**Subheadline:** "Camping, grilling, and off-road essentials — tested for real adventures, shipped to your door."
**CTA button:** "Shop Best Sellers"

**Value props row (3 icons):**
- 🚚 Fast, tracked shipping

## 5. Apps to install (all have free tiers)
- **CJdropshipping** (official Shopify app) — product import + order fulfillment sync
- **Judge.me** or **Loox** — product reviews with photos (critical for trust in dropshipping)
- **Klaviyo** or Shopify Email — abandoned cart + welcome email flow
- **Privy** or built-in Shopify pop-up — email capture for 10% off

## 6. Product listing checklist (per product, when importing from CJ)
- [ ] **Tracked inventory only** — skip listings with unknown/null stock; enable Shopify inventory tracking (`inventory_management = shopify`) with a known quantity (team rule: `RULES.md` §5)
- [ ] Rewrite the title (avoid copy-pasted supplier titles — make them benefit-driven, e.g. "Solar Rechargeable Camping Lantern — 3 Light Modes")
- [ ] Rewrite the description in your brand voice; lead with the problem it solves
- [ ] Use at least 4–6 clean product images (CJ provides these; crop/clean as needed)
- [ ] Set price using a **2.5–3.5x markup** over CJ cost to cover ads, returns, and margin (standard dropshipping benchmark)
- [ ] Add to the correct collection(s) and tag for search/filtering
- [ ] Set weight/shipping profile correctly to avoid shipping-cost losses

## 7. Payments & legal
- Set up **Shopify Payments** (or PayPal as backup) — requires your real business/bank info, done directly in Shopify admin.
- Confirm your Privacy/Refund/Shipping policies mention dropshipping lead times honestly (builds trust, reduces chargebacks).

## 8. Launch checklist
- [ ] 12–18 hero products live (see `product-research.md` shortlist)
- [ ] At least 2 bundles created
- [ ] Homepage, About, FAQ, Contact, legal pages published
- [ ] Payments enabled and tested with a $0 test order (Shopify's Bogus Gateway) or a real $1 test purchase you refund
- [ ] Email capture pop-up + welcome discount flow active
- [ ] Reviews app installed (import CJ product reviews if available)
- [ ] Google Analytics / Shopify Analytics + Meta Pixel connected for tracking
- [ ] Soft-launch: share with friends/family, fix any checkout issues, then start small ad budget ($5–10/day) to validate

## 9. What requires the human (cannot be done by this agent)
- Logging into Shopify/CJdropshipping (no credentials provided to this agent)
- Any purchase (product samples, apps, domain, ad spend) — explicitly excluded per task instructions
- Connecting bank/payment details
- Approving legal policy text as final

## 10. Metrics to track post-launch (for the "profitable" goal)
- Conversion rate (industry-wide new-dropshipping-store benchmark is roughly 1.5–3%; actual results vary a lot by traffic source/niche/pricing, so treat this as a rough starting reference, not a guarantee)
- Average order value (boost via bundles/upsells)
- Customer acquisition cost vs. margin per order (must stay below your product margin)
- Return/refund rate by product (cut underperformers after 30–60 days of data)
