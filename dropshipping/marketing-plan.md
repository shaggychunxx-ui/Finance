### Dayparting

At micro-budget, **do not** daypart heavily (Meta will starve). Run **all day** until you have ≥2 weeks of data.

- Give a creative **at least $8–12** before judging.  
- If CTR &lt; 0.6% (Meta feed) or no ATC after ~$12 → replace creative, not necessarily the product.  
- If ATC but no purchase → check price, shipping copy, mobile checkout, and trust badges.

## 5. Creative playbook (cheap production)

### Formats that work at low budget

1. **UGC-style phone video (15–25s):** unbox → use → result. Text overlays.  
2. **Before/after stills:** dark camp → lantern on; wet phone risk → dry pouch.  
3. **Pack-out carousel:** “Weekend camp in 5 items” → swipe to product.  
4. **Static with bold benefit:** “Flat tire on a forest road? Fix it in minutes.”

### Copy formulas

- **Hook:** problem in first line.  
- **Proof:** one concrete feature (lumens, capacity, waterproof, dB reduction).  
- **Trust:** “Tracked shipping · 30-day returns.”  
- **CTA:** Shop now / Get yours.

### Brand voice

Rugged, practical, no luxury fluff. Honest about **7–18 day** standard shipping; call out **fast-ship** items when true.

### Creative testing order (per hero)

1. One **problem/solution video**  
2. One **static lifestyle**  
3. One **feature close-up**  

Kill losers; scale winners by **duplicating ad set**, not by blasting budget overnight.

## 8. Metrics that matter (micro-budget scoreboard)

| Metric | Healthy early signal | Action if bad |
|--------|----------------------|---------------|
| **CTR (link)** | ≥ 1.0% Meta feed | New hook/creative |
| **CPC** | &lt; $1.00 ideal; &lt; $1.50 ok early | Tighten creative or audience |
| **ATC rate** | Any ATCs in first $15 | If 0 → landing or offer issue |
| **CPA / purchase** | Must be **&lt; contribution margin** | Pause if spend &gt; 1× margin with 0 sales after solid traffic |
| **ROAS** | Aim ≥ 1.5 once optimized; early may be &lt;1 while learning | Judge over **2 weeks**, not 1 day |
| **Return rate** | Watch per SKU | Kill high-return products from ads |

### Contribution margin reminder

Retail − product cost − shipping − payment fees − returns buffer = max CAC.  
If ads CPA &gt; that number, the SKU is not ready to scale.
